### Body structure — suggested

A shape worth starting from, and worth departing from when the skill doesn't
fit it:

```markdown
---
name: {name}
description: {when to use it, with real trigger phrases}
---

# {Readable title}

{One line: what this does and when it comes into play}

## When NOT to use this

## {Main section — the what}

## Process / flow

## Standard output
```

The one section worth keeping even when you drop the rest is **When NOT to use
this**. Overlap between skills is the most expensive failure in a growing
collection, and it's the section that prevents it.

Two sections to add only when there's real risk of a step being skipped —
audits, verification, technical verdicts:

- **Rationalizations** — excuses for cutting a corner, each with a counter
  specific to this skill.
- **Red flags** — signals, mid-execution, that the result is going wrong.
